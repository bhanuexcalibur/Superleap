Strategic Diagnostic

Three GTM friction points worth testing.

Core assumptions on why modern revenue leaders actively explore alternatives to traditional CRMs.

Market Hypothesis 01

Reps Resent Manual Data Entry

Legacy CRMs were designed primarily for management reporting, turning account executives into unpaid data entry administrators.

PMM Play: Position zero-touch logging as rep productivity unlock.
Market Hypothesis 02

Add-On AI Lacks Workflow Agency

Incumbents bolt on conversational chatbot wrappers that can summarize notes but cannot proactively take operational action.

PMM Play: Lead with autonomous SuperAgents over passive assistants.
Market Hypothesis 03

Heavy Implementation Overhead

Enterprise rollouts typically require extensive systems-integrator consulting before reaching baseline day-to-day utility.

PMM Play: Highlight rapid onboarding and pre-built vertical logic.
